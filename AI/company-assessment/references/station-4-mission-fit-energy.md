# Station 4: Mission Fit — Energy

## Objective
Assess alignment with the Energy transition mission (net-zero technologies, renewables, supply chain decarbonization, and clean energy innovation).

## Primary Inputs Evaluated
- Company Name & SIC Code (Fields 1 & 5)
- Company operations, products/services, and sector context

## Scoring Logic (0–10)
*Replace or refine the criteria below with your exact organizational rules:*

- **0–3 (Red — No / Minimal Alignment):**
  - Core business has no substantive link to clean energy, decarbonization, or energy transition supply chains (or relies heavily on high-emission activities without transition plans).
- **4–7 (Amber — Indirect / Enabling Fit):**
  - Adjacent technology, engineering, or digital service provider whose capabilities can transfer into or support the energy transition supply chain.
- **8–10 (Green — Direct Mission Fit):**
  - Core business directly delivers clean energy generation, storage, grid infrastructure, hydrogen, offshore wind, or industrial decarbonization solutions.
- **Not Checked:**
  - Use only if explicitly instructed to skip Station 4.

## Output Requirements
1. **Score:** Integer `0–10` (or `Not Checked`).
2. **Report:** Concise assessment of direct or supply-chain contribution to the Energy mission.
