# Station 2: Company Characteristics

## Objective
Assess the company's structural profile, sector classification, size, and operational maturity.

## Primary Inputs Evaluated
- SME Status (Field 3)
- SIC Code (Field 5)
- Turnover & International Sales Turnover (Fields 9 & 10)
- Total Headcount & Scottish Headcount (Fields 11 & 12)

## Scoring Logic (0–10)
*Replace or refine the criteria below with your exact organizational rules:*

- **0–3 (Red — Weak / Misaligned Profile):**
  - Operating in an excluded or low-priority SIC sector, or structural metrics (headcount/turnover) indicate minimal operational substance.
- **4–7 (Amber — Standard / Developing Profile):**
  - Valid commercial profile in an acceptable SIC code; moderate headcount/turnover or partial data provided (e.g., SIC or turnover left blank).
- **8–10 (Green — Strong Target Profile):**
  - High-value SIC sector alignment, strong revenue-per-head metrics, and clear operational maturity appropriate to its `SME Status`.
- **Not Checked:**
  - Use only if explicitly instructed to skip Station 2.

## Output Requirements
1. **Score:** Integer `0–10` (or `Not Checked`).
2. **Report:** Concise analysis of sector classification (SIC), organizational scale, and operational resilience.
