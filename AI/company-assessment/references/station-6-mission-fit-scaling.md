# Station 6: Mission Fit — Scaling

## Objective
Assess the company's readiness and capability to scale rapidly in revenue, international markets, and high-value headcount.

## Primary Inputs Evaluated
- International Category & SME Status (Fields 2 & 3)
- Turnover & International Sales Turnover (Fields 9 & 10)
- Total Headcount & Scottish Headcount (Fields 11 & 12)
- Primary Contact / Leadership context (Field 6)

## Scoring Logic (0–10)
*Replace or refine the criteria below with your exact organizational rules:*

- **0–3 (Red — Lifestyle / Non-Scaling Model):**
  - Operating model is locally bounded, capacity-constrained, or lacks repeatable growth mechanisms.
- **4–7 (Amber — Early Scaling / Operational Bottlenecks):**
  - Proven product-market fit with growing headcount or international sales, but scaling is currently constrained by leadership bandwidth, supply chain, or process maturity.
- **8–10 (Green — High-Velocity Scale-Up Fit):**
  - Repeatable, high-margin commercial model with accelerating international sales, strong leadership capacity, and clear trajectory to become a major scaling enterprise.
- **Not Checked:**
  - Use only if explicitly instructed to skip Station 6.

## Output Requirements
1. **Score:** Integer `0–10` (or `Not Checked`).
2. **Report:** Concise evaluation of scaling bottlenecks, repeatable growth drivers, and international expansion readiness.
