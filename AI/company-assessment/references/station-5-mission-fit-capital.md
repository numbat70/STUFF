# Station 5: Mission Fit — Capital

## Objective
Assess investment readiness, capital absorption capacity, and alignment with Capital mission objectives (attracting private/institutional investment and co-investment leverage).

## Primary Inputs Evaluated
- International Category & SME Status (Fields 2 & 3)
- Turnover & International Sales Turnover (Fields 9 & 10)
- Total Headcount (Field 11)

## Scoring Logic (0–10)
*Replace or refine the criteria below with your exact organizational rules:*

- **0–3 (Red — Not Investment Ready / Low Leverage):**
  - Unclear commercial model, weak financial governance indicators, or minimal capacity to attract or deploy growth capital.
- **4–7 (Amber — Developing Capital Fit):**
  - Viable commercial traction with potential for future equity/debt or capital expenditure (`FDI` / `SDI Prospect`), requiring further financial due diligence or readiness support.
- **8–10 (Green — High Capital Fit & Leverage):**
  - Strong investment proposition, clear capital deployment plan, and high potential to crowd in private/international capital into Scotland.
- **Not Checked:**
  - Use only if explicitly instructed to skip Station 5.

## Output Requirements
1. **Score:** Integer `0–10` (or `Not Checked`).
2. **Report:** Concise evaluation of capital readiness, investment attractiveness, and co-investment potential.
