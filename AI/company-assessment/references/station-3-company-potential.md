# Station 3: Company Potential

## Objective
Evaluate commercial growth trajectory, international trade capability, and economic impact potential in Scotland.

## Primary Inputs Evaluated
- International Category (Field 2)
- Turnover & International Sales Turnover (Fields 9 & 10)
- Total Headcount & Scottish Headcount (Fields 11 & 12)

## Scoring Logic (0–10)
*Replace or refine the criteria below with your exact organizational rules:*

- **0–3 (Red — Low Growth / Domestic-Only Stagnation):**
  - Little to no international sales traction, flat/declining indicators, or minimal capacity for job/GVA growth.
- **4–7 (Amber — Moderate / Emerging Potential):**
  - Early export activity (`Turnover from International Sales` < 25% of total) or credible expansion plans, constrained by resource gaps or missing financial data.
- **8–10 (Green — High Growth & Export Potential):**
  - Strong international revenue share, clear competitive differentiation, and high potential for Scottish job creation or export expansion.
- **Not Checked:**
  - Use only if explicitly instructed to skip Station 3.

## Output Requirements
1. **Score:** Integer `0–10` (or `Not Checked`).
2. **Report:** Concise evaluation of export readiness, commercial scalability, and economic value potential.
